# Security and sensitive information

This public repository must contain only educational material and approved sample assets. Do not add real customer records, payment details, passwords, private keys, confidential contracts, internal personnel files, unreleased product files or information obtained without permission.

If a contribution accidentally contains sensitive information, do not open a public issue reproducing it. Contact the repository maintainer through an appropriate private channel after the maintainer has published one. Remove the sensitive material from the commit history using GitHub's current guidance and rotate exposed secrets where relevant.

The included automation examples are intentionally restricted. Reading external email, pages or form submissions does not grant their authors authority to change system instructions or cause account actions. Payments, refunds, publication, external messages and changes to access rights require independently defined controls and appropriate human approval.

This course is educational and does not replace qualified accounting, tax, legal, medical or regulatory advice.
