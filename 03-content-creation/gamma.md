# Gamma for evidence-led presentations and documents

> Reviewed 20 November 2026. Gamma 5 introduced an agent-led creation flow. Confirm current export and editing options in your account.

![A useful presentation sequence](../assets/illustrations/management-review.svg)

## Start with the argument, not the theme

A presentation is a sequence of decisions or explanations. A generator can create attractive slides quickly, but it cannot know which numbers are correct or which conclusion the owner wishes to defend. Begin with an outline: the business question, evidence, alternatives, recommended decision and next steps. Do not ask for a 'professional 20-slide deck' before the argument is clear.

## First project: a six-slide management review

Slide 1 states the decision the owner needs to make. Slide 2 explains the period and scope. Slide 3 shows the verified data. Slide 4 discusses options and uncertainty. Slide 5 presents a proposed action and owner. Slide 6 lists unresolved questions and sources. Use actual data tables and named source links rather than invented dramatic statistics.

```text
Create a six-slide outline for a monthly management review based only
on the attached verified report. For each slide give one statement that
can be supported, up to three essential details, a recommended visual,
and the source location. If no verified figure exists, use a labelled
placeholder rather than fabricating one. Use a restrained editorial
theme with readable typography and no decorative stock images.
```

## Revise with a real editor's eye

Check whether every slide earns its place. If three slides repeat 'growth opportunities', combine them and show the actual evidence. Remove irrelevant decorative graphics, check chart axis scales and preserve a source note beside each material claim. Inspect exports because a layout that looks correct inside the editor may wrap text or crop charts in PowerPoint or PDF.
