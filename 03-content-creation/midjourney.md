## Core guidance from the expanded handbook

*Visual exploration and artistic image direction*

### Where this tool earns its place

V8.2 is documented as the current default version in Midjourney. It is well suited to exploring photographic mood, composition and artistic treatments. A moodboard is not proof that a product, person or place exists.

### Before the first prompt

Start with a short visual premise rather than a long list of flattering adjectives. Decide on subject, place, lens or framing, light, material and aspect ratio. Use authorised image references where useful, and keep a record of which seed, style or version produced the frame if the interface exposes it.

### A complete business exercise

Create a moodboard for a homeware brand with four distinct compositions: tabletop detail, open shelf, craft process and portrait of a work environment. Select a common palette and light quality. Reject scenes that imply a false actual workshop or staff member. Use the moodboard to brief a real photographer or build illustrative campaign backgrounds.

### Copy ready prompt for this tool

> Editorial interior photograph, handmade woven baskets resting on open timber shelves in a modest daylight filled shop, one basket partly cropped in foreground, warm off white walls, quiet earthy palette, truthful material texture, natural shadow gradient, 35 mm documentary photographic framing, subtle imperfections, no people, no labels, no futuristic light strips, horizontal composition.

### When the result is weak

If the image is spectacular but unhelpful, simplify the shot. Ask what a shopper needs to see about material, scale and use. Remove the words “cinematic masterpiece”, “ultra premium” and similar mood inflation.

### Human editorial finish

Art direction should reveal a particular place or material. If the campaign claims to show the actual shop, photograph the actual shop rather than displaying a fictional render.

### Approval boundary and common errors

Confirm V8.2 access and variation controls, visual rights and truthful labelling of synthetic environments.

**Official reference:** [Midjourney V8.2 product guidance](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32199405667853-Version)

## First session: from a blank workspace to a reviewable result

1. Define the intended visual world in concrete terms: place, materials, time of day, use and framing.
2. Work from a few subject and lighting references, respecting the rights of each source.
3. Generate separate composition studies rather than changing every variable at once.
4. Record selected seed or style settings if supported by the interface.
5. Lay out chosen frames together to detect repeating poses, colour or camera angles.
6. Use the moodboard to direct actual photography or clearly labelled illustrative artwork.

## Prompt ladder: simple, evidence based and advanced

The prompts below are teaching examples. Replace fictional context with approved records. A tool that does not support freeform prompting may require you to perform the equivalent steps through its own interface.

### First pass

```text
Propose four distinct photographic compositions for a handmade homewares story: shelf, material detail, process table and room context. Explain what each contributes.
```

### Visual prompt

```text
Natural daylight inside a small homeware shop, cane baskets on weathered timber shelves, slightly asymmetric composition, true woven texture, ordinary room shadows, 35 mm documentary framing, no people or signs.
```

### Refinement

```text
Keep the natural material textures, but move from a heroic low angle to eye level. Reduce exaggerated warm colour grading and leave realistic empty shelf space.
```

## What an acceptable result looks like

A good moodboard tells the photographer what to notice. It does not invent an actual studio or prove that a product was handmade in the depicted place.

## If the first version disappoints

If all frames resemble a luxury hotel advertisement, remove prestige adjectives and add actual environmental references. If patterns repeat, change framing purpose rather than adding random objects.

## Human editorial finish

A brand’s visual character often lies in its materials, customers, work surface and real setting. Preserve these instead of polishing every trace away.

## Practice assignment

Using a fictional or appropriately protected version of the case, complete **build a distinctive editorial moodboard**. Save an evidence inventory, the first draft, a precise correction request and the reviewed deliverable. Write a short comparison showing what the revision genuinely improved.

## Approval and success test

Can the art director explain why each chosen frame belongs to the same story while doing a distinct job?

## Art direction lab: references, restraint and V8.2

Midjourney is often used for concept development, editorial imagery and mood exploration. Start by describing a scene that can be photographed, not by piling up style adjectives. Its July 2026 V8.2 update introduced a new Edit Model; some older tutorials on Omni Reference and Character Reference no longer describe the preferred workflow in V8.2. Consult the current model compatibility chart before copying parameters from an older prompt.

**Create a small visual territory.** A fictional ceramic studio wants a campaign that feels tactile and quiet. Use five reference words: daylight, hand-thrown clay, warm wall, negative space, irregular finish. Generate only exploratory concepts at first. Review whether they express the brand without borrowing a competitor's identity.

```text
Editorial photograph of three hand-thrown ceramic cups on a worn
wooden shelf in a modest daylight pottery studio. Slight differences
in glaze and rim shape. Soft side lighting from an off-frame window,
subtle environmental shadows, calm muted colours, realistic material
texture, natural camera perspective, enough negative space for later
layout. No chrome, glowing particles, dramatic science-fiction hues,
brand logo, printed claim, crowd of props or impossible reflections.
--ar 4:5
```

Use only parameters your current Midjourney version supports. Keep a generation log noting version, references, seed when applicable and the editorial reason for selecting an image. For an actual product, use photos of the product instead of treating a generated object as accurate stock photography.

**Improve specificity.** Replace *cinematic* with the actual camera height and light source; replace *luxury* with material and composition; replace *aesthetic* with the three visual choices that matter. A distinctive style arises from deliberate constraints, not from more adjectives.
