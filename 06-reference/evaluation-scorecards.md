# Quality scorecards for decisions, writing, design, video and voice

A numerical score is useful only when the reviewers agree what is being measured. Each rubric below is a coaching aid, not a claim of objective machine detection. In particular, no score proves whether material was generated or written by a human.

## Written communication, 0 to 20

| Criterion | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Truth | Invented or contradicted facts | Minor ambiguity | Every material claim supported |
| Reader usefulness | No clear problem solved | Partially useful | Answers an actual question |
| Voice | Generic promotion | Mixed voice | Recognisable, appropriate wording |
| Structure | Repetitive or confusing | Mostly clear | Natural and easy to follow |
| Action | Missing or misleading | Somewhat clear | Accurate and easy to take |

A score below 16 should trigger review of the weak criteria, not automatic regeneration of the whole piece. A single critical false claim blocks publication regardless of total score.

## Poster, 0 to 20

Check message hierarchy, visual truthfulness, readability, brand fit and factual approval on the same 0, 2, 4 scale. A generated poster with a stunning background but invented product detail fails truthfulness even if the other four measures score highly.

## Video, 0 to 20

Check observable storyline, factual integrity, motion continuity, sound clarity and caption accuracy. Watch once with sound off and once while looking away from the screen. These tests reveal whether the visuals and narration each communicate the essential information.

## Voice, 0 to 20

Check pronunciation, natural pacing, emotional fit, intelligibility and permission to use the voice. A legally or ethically unauthorised imitation is not publishable, even if the pronunciation score is perfect.

## Business report, 0 to 20

Check source provenance, arithmetic reconciliation, definitions, uncertainty handling and decision usefulness. If profit is reported without cost data, the report is not acceptable regardless of presentation quality.

## Prompt evaluation record

```text
TASK:
SOURCE AND VERSION:
MODEL OR APPLICATION AND VERSION IF KNOWN:
INITIAL RESULT:
FAILED CRITERION:
EVIDENCE OF FAILURE:
ONE CHANGE TO REQUEST:
REVISED RESULT:
HUMAN APPROVAL:
NEXT EXPERIMENT:
```

Store personal details only in the organisation's approved system. Use fictional records for examples in a public repository.
