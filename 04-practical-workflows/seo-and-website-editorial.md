# Website content, product pages and responsible search visibility

A website should answer a real person's question better than competing pages, not repeat variations of a phrase to fill a keyword table. Google's Search Central guidance emphasises useful, reliable content for people and cautions against producing pages at scale merely to manipulate rankings or generative answer surfaces.

## Before drafting

Map the customer intent: finding the business, comparing a service, deciding whether to purchase or seeking support. Collect accurate product specifications, business hours, service areas, contact details, return policy and genuine photographs. For services, explain what happens during an appointment and who provides it. Avoid unverifiable superlatives, fake reviews and unapproved client stories.

```text
Draft a service page for a fictional independent bicycle repair shop.
The supplied facts list only three services, working hours, address,
pricing method and booking contact. Write a clear page title, a short
opening that describes the service, separate sections answering three
actual customer questions, and one next step. Do not invent prices,
qualifications, emergency availability or customer testimonials.
Provide a list of facts to verify before publication.
```

## Editorial quality review

Read the page as a prospective customer. Can they identify the service area, the exact offer and the booking step quickly? Does any sentence exist only to repeat a search term? Does the page include first-hand evidence such as a real process photograph or an approved specialist explanation? Can important claims be attributed? Fix these before writing more content.

## Technical review

Use descriptive headings, readable links, accessible images and accurate metadata. Check the page on mobile, ensure forms work, and keep business details consistent. Verify the live page after publication; the editing preview is not the final customer experience. Track meaningful enquiries and qualified traffic rather than promising search rank improvements.

**References:** [Helpful people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [Search guidance on generated content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content).
